# Privacy Policy for FOR YOU

**Last updated: September 1st, 2026**

FOR YOU ("the App") is a personal gift-planning app developed by Mewie. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

## The short version

**We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your data to any server. Everything you enter into the App — names, dates, gift ideas, budgets, preferences — stays only on your own device.**

## What information the App stores

When you use FOR YOU, you may enter:

- Names, birthdays, and anniversaries of people you know
- Personal preferences and gift ideas for those people
- Budget amounts and gift purchase history
- Your chosen app theme and settings

All of this information is stored **locally on your device only**, using your device's local storage. It is never sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. We do not operate any backend server for this App, and we have no way to access your data.

## Local notifications

The App may ask for permission to send local notifications to remind you of upcoming birthdays or anniversaries. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device by the Android operating system. No notification data is sent to us or anyone else.

## Backup and export

The App includes an optional "Export Backup" feature that lets you save your data as a file. This file is created and handled entirely through your device's own sharing/saving system (for example, saving it to your files or sending it via an app you choose). We do not receive a copy of this file, and where it goes is entirely up to you.

## Third parties

The App does not include any advertising, analytics, or tracking software. We do not share your information with any third party, because we never receive it in the first place.

## Data deletion

You can delete all data stored by the App at any time using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings, or by uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data along with it.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children. Since the App does not collect or transmit any information at all, this is true regardless of the user's age.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if new features are added that change how data is handled), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
